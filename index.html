Ol Oret Grumeti Luxury Lodge Robanda · Serengeti

Set in the heart of Robanda, on the western corridor of the Serengeti ecosystem, Ol-Oret Grumeti is a sanctuary inspired by the timeless rhythm of nature. The name Ol-Oret, meaning "the never-ending circle," reflects the eternal cycle of life that defines the Serengeti — where wildlife, the changing seasons, and the world-renowned Great Migration continue in an unbroken rhythm year after year.

Perfectly positioned within the migration route, Ol-Oret Grumeti offers guests an extraordinary opportunity to witness one of the world's greatest natural spectacles as hundreds of thousands of wildebeest, zebras, and gazelles journey across the plains in search of fresh grazing. Here, every season reveals a different chapter of the Serengeti's enduring story, ensuring that no two safaris are ever the same.

Designed to blend seamlessly with its surroundings, the lodge combines refined luxury with authentic African character. Spacious tented suites, thoughtfully crafted from natural materials, provide elegant comfort while framing uninterrupted views of the surrounding wilderness. Every detail — from locally inspired cuisine and personalized service to tranquil wellness experiences and carefully curated safari adventures — has been created to immerse guests in the beauty and spirit of the Serengeti.

At Ol-Oret Grumeti, every sunrise heralds a new adventure, every sunset celebrates the wilderness, and every stay becomes part of nature's never-ending circle. More than a destination, it is an invitation to experience the Serengeti as it has existed for millennia — wild, timeless, and unforgettable.

At Ol-Oret Chemchem, every encounter with wildlife, every quiet moment beneath ancient baobabs, and every journey along the elephant corridor becomes part of nature's never-ending circle. It is a place where luxury and conservation meet, offering an unforgettable safari experience rooted in the timeless spirit of Tanzania's wild landscapes.

Serengeti · Robanda

Explore The Ol-Oret Grumeti

Located in the wildlife-rich plains of Robanda on the north-western edge of Serengeti National Park, Ol-Oret Grumeti offers an exceptional safari escape where luxury meets one of the most dramatic wildlife landscapes on earth.

The lodge is strategically positioned just a few kilometers (15 minutes' drive) from the Serengeti Ikoma entrance gate, one of the main access points into the Serengeti's western corridor and central plains. Serengeti Ikoma Gate sits close to Robanda and provides quick access for daily game drives into the park's wildlife-rich ecosystems.

The lodge is also conveniently located near Ikoma Airstrip, allowing guests to arrive by scenic flight from Arusha in about one to two hours before a short transfer to the lodge. From the lodge, guests can reach the heart of the Serengeti within a short drive, while also enjoying the benefits of being outside the park where exclusive activities such as walking safaris and night game drives are possible.

The area around Robanda lies directly along the route of the legendary Great Migration, when millions of wildebeest and Plains zebra move across the Serengeti ecosystem.

Within driving distance to the north lies the dramatic Mara River, where between July and October the migration reaches its most iconic moment as massive herds gather and attempt the dangerous river crossings watched by predators and crocodiles.

Because of this unique location, guests staying at Ol Oret Grumeti Luxury Lodge are perfectly positioned to explore three major safari regions:

  • The Western Serengeti corridor, known for migration routes and the Grumeti River ecosystem
  • The Central Serengeti plains, famous for year-round predators and big cat sightings
  • The Northern Serengeti, home to the dramatic Mara River migration crossings

Accommodation

Our lodge features 16 elegant permanent safari tents, inclusive of family tents carefully designed to blend seamlessly with the surrounding landscape. Each tent rests on beautiful stone flooring and is accented with natural stone décor, creating a warm and timeless connection to the environment. At Ol-Oret Grumeti, luxury is defined not only by comfort but by a genuine connection to nature, community, and the Serengeti landscape. Here, every moment invites you to slow down, breathe deeply, and experience the magic of Africa in its most authentic form.

Experiences

Wildlife Safaris and Game Drives

Enjoy the game drives in the Serengeti all year around. The unique positioning of our lodge enables you to explore the central Serengeti game viewing and the great migration.

Night Game Drives

An exclusive after-dark safari revealing the hidden, nocturnal side of the Serengeti — where predators hunt and the wilderness truly comes alive.

Walking Safaris

Guided bush walks (where permitted, often in nearby conservancies), bird watching and photography experiences and exploring the remote western Serengeti ecosystem (Robanda area) known for resident wildlife year-round.

Cultural & Maasai Village Visits

Visits to nearby Maasai communities and learning about local traditions, lifestyle, and conservation.

Bush Breakfast

Enjoy a beautiful breakfast in the bush after an early game drive in the morning.

Sundowner Cocktails and Campfire

Enjoy a glass of wine and cocktails under the skies after an afternoon game drive. One of the safari highlights is sitting around the camp fire, listening to the sounds of the wild, watching the sun go down and watching the never-ending sky.

Hot Air Balloon Safari

A hot air balloon safari over the Serengeti National Park is a breathtaking sunrise experience where guests float silently above the plains, spotting wildlife from a unique aerial view. After landing, the adventure ends with a champagne bush breakfast, served in the wild under acacia trees — combining luxury, exclusivity, and unforgettable scenery in one experience. This experience starts at 4.30am and the safari in the sky is 50 to 90 minutes depending on the weather.

Massage and Spa

After a day on a game drive, pamper yourself with a relaxing and rejuvenating massage. There is a choice of a 1 hour 30 minutes body massage, a head massage, a manicure or pedicure.

Gym & Fitness

Keep up your routine in the wild — a fully equipped gym with modern cardio and strength equipment, open daily for guests before or after time on safari.
