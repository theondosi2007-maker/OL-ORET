Welcome to Ol Oret

Two lodges, one unbroken rhythm

Ol Oret Luxury Lodges are inspired by the timeless cycles of nature, where wildlife, the changing seasons, and life itself continue in an unbroken rhythm. Rooted in the spirit of Tanzania, Ol Oret Luxury Lodges celebrate the enduring beauty of the wilderness and the profound connection between people and nature.

Ol Oret Grumeti Luxury Lodge

Robanda · Serengeti

Set in the heart of Robanda, on the western corridor of the Serengeti ecosystem, Ol-Oret Grumeti is a sanctuary inspired by the timeless rhythm of nature. The name Ol-Oret, meaning "the never-ending circle," reflects the eternal cycle of life that defines the Serengeti, where wildlife, the changing seasons, and the world-renowned Great Migration continue in an unbroken rhythm year after year.

Perfectly positioned within the migration route, Ol-Oret Grumeti offers guests an extraordinary opportunity to witness one of the world's greatest natural spectacles as hundreds of thousands of wildebeest, zebras, and gazelles journey across the plains in search of fresh grazing.

Ol Oret Chemchem Luxury Lodge

Lake Manyara · Tarangire

Situated near one of Tanzania's most important elephant corridors, the lodge offers guests a rare opportunity to witness these magnificent giants following the same ancestral routes they have travelled for centuries. As elephants move between the forests of Lake Manyara and the open plains of Tarangire in search of food, water, and sanctuary, they remind us that nature's greatest stories are written not in a single season, but across generations.

Designed to exist in harmony with its surroundings, Ol-Oret Chemchem combines understated luxury with an authentic sense of place. Elegant accommodations, inspired by the beauty of the African landscape, offer comfort, privacy, and uninterrupted views of the surrounding wilderness.

Getting there

By air (recommended)

Serengeti: 40-minute charter flight to Seronera Airstrip. Manyara: 1-hour charter from Arusha. We arrange transfers and coordinate all details.

By road

Serengeti: 4–5 hour scenic drive. Manyara: 2 hours from Arusha. Roads are generally good; allow extra time in rainy season.

Nearest airports

Kilimanjaro International (KIA): 60km from Arusha. Arusha Airport: 30km from Arusha. Charter or overland from either.
